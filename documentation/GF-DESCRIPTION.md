---

Akhil Notes is a handwriting typeface developed from the personal notebooks of designer Akhil Desai. Originally created to bring the familiarity of handwritten notes into digital environments, it preserves the natural irregularities, expressive strokes and rhythm of everyday handwriting.

The family combines an informal, personal character with the versatility of a functional typeface. Its extended character coverage includes accented Latin letters, Vietnamese characters, mathematical notation, currencies, fractions, arrows and utility symbols, making it suitable for educational content, journaling, annotations, presentations and everyday digital writing.

Available in weights from Regular to Black with matching italics, Akhil Notes brings a human quality to digital communication while offering flexibility for structured and expressive typography.

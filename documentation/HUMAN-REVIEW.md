- [ ] Weight ramp recognizability (especially constructed Æ/Œ/ß/Þ/ð, currencies) — [`OPTICAL-REVIEW.md`](OPTICAL-REVIEW.md)

- [ ] Device matrix: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Figma, browsers

FONTLOG for Akhil Notes
-----------------------

This file provides detailed information on the Akhil Notes Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Akhil Notes fonts
and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Akhil Notes is a handwriting family developed from the personal notebooks
of designer Akhil Desai. It preserves natural irregularities of everyday
handwriting while supporting notes, education, annotations and everyday
digital writing.

Supported glyphsets (glyphsets 1.1.3): GF_Latin_Core, GF_Latin_Plus,
GF_Latin_Vietnamese, plus mathematical, currency and utility symbols.

Axes: wght 400–900. Italic is a separate family file (not a slant axis on
the Google Fonts variable pair).
